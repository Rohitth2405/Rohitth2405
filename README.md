## Research Interests

My interests include computational methods for understanding and
improving aerospace systems, particularly:

-   Aerodynamic and aeroelastic behaviour of variable-sweep wings
-   CFD-based prediction of lift, drag, pitching moment, and
    aerodynamic-centre movement
-   Flutter, gust response, and unsteady aerodynamic loads
-   Machine-learning surrogate models for faster aerospace design
    studies
-   Propulsion systems and multidisciplinary aerospace research

One of my developing research directions combines **CAD, CFD
simulations, and machine-learning-based surrogate modelling** to study
how wing sweep affects aerodynamic and aeroelastic performance.
