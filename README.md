## Research Interests

- Aerodynamic and aeroelastic behaviour of variable-sweep wings

- Flutter, gust response and unsteady aerodynamic loads
- Machine-learning surrogate models for aerospace design
- Propulsion systems and multidisciplinary aerospace research

My developing research direction combines **CAD, CFD simulations and machine-learning-based surrogate modelling** to investigate how wing sweep affects aerodynamic and aeroelastic performance.
